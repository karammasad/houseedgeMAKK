# Flip-time posterior calibration (bounded diagnostic) -- NO fixable inference problem found
Production gate (v8/v9 + shared-base) posterior over (structure, flip-time grid + no flip), existing fitted worlds 1 and 2, 2,270 fresh held-out simulated GATED machines (1,125 flipped). No refit, no production edit, no audit.
Pre-checks: production does expose the joint posterior (G.machine_tables + flip prior; tau grid step 2 from 40 to L-3). Simulator flip process = the model's own prior (p_change .5, uniform tau), so this tests inference, not the prior. REAL prevalence check: production says 66.8% of real long train machines are flipped (P>.9) vs 49.2% in the simulator, so the simulator under-represents flips (real p_change looks nearer .67). Posteriors are decisive on both (share between .1 and .9: .3% real, 1.0% sim), so the prior mismatch is negligible for long logs.
Calibration of P(flipped): bins calibrated at the ends and in [.30,.50) (.439 vs .429); [.50,.70) predicted .649 vs observed .435 (n=223, CI .37-.50); slope .685. Brier .0611.
Flip time: long logs: 86.6% of posterior mass within +-2 ticks of the true tau, 90% credible-set coverage .917 (nominal .90), median width 2 ticks. Short layout: coverage .887, mass .16, width 144 ticks: honest but diffuse.
Error attribution (five-category x1e-4 vs truth, total 54.7): confident-right 1,740 machines 21.6% of error; confidently WRONG 10 machines (0.4%) 40.3%; diffuse 520 machines 38.1%. Of the 10: 7 flips in the last 1-6 ticks (undetectable; the prior itself gives such flips ~2% of machines), 2 false flips, 1 missed old flip. Late flips (<=8 ticks) are 4.9% of flipped machines, as the prior predicts.
Knowing flipped-or-not removes 31.5 [14.3, 52.7] (58%); knowing tau removes 34.4.
Fixability test: Platt recalibration of P(flipped), cross-fitted by episode half: error rises, +2.47 [+0.65, +4.35] (long +1.66 [-0.21, +3.73], short +4.96 [+1.27, +9.42]). The mid-bin miscalibration is not an exploitable pattern.
Verdict: NO-GO / no evidence of a fixable inference problem. Remaining flip error is irreducible (flips too close to the cutoff, diffuse short layouts) and the posterior is honest about it. Minor structural note: grid stops at L-3, so flips at L-2/L-1 are unrepresentable (about .5% of machines; hedging them is worth <0.1e-4). Caveat: in-family simulator; says nothing about real-data misspecification.
